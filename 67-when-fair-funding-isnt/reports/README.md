# Research reports

Maintain the research log and final evidence required by `PROMPT.md` here or at the repository root when the prompt specifies exact paths. Typical artifacts include prior art, preregistration, methods, raw-result manifests, results, negative results, limitations, replication instructions, claim ledgers, paper drafts, and next steps.

Do not fill these documents with plans alone. Link central claims to executable code, configurations, tests, and machine-readable evidence.
