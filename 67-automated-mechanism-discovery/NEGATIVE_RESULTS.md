# Negative Results

The original frozen domain has no accepted strict welfare improver over the
anonymous-OR baseline. The three-agent extension adds a wider bounded transfer
grid and still finds no accepted nonzero-transfer rule or welfare improver over
pointwise-optimal majority. These are finite negative results for their stated
classes, not an impossibility theorem for unrestricted mechanisms.

Within the frozen finite space, no accepted mechanism strictly improves `anonymous_or` under uniform allocative welfare. The accepted set has empty intersection with neutrality. Serial dictatorship fails the frozen anonymity/fairness requirement; VCG supplies the expected DSIC/IR/feasibility comparator but fails exact budget balance. None of these finite negatives is a general impossibility theorem.

For the displayed PRIMA-2016 Equation (3) correction in the normalized
three-agent continuous audit, retained efficiency reaches `-47/162` at
`(0,0,1)` while budget slack remains `64/81`. This is an exact property of the
printed formula and declared normalization, and must not be read as a
refutation of a different competitive criterion or asymptotic result.
